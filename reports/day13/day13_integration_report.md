## 3. Complete Video Processing Pipeline Integration

The pipeline connects 12 sequential stages without duplication:
1. **Video Ingestion & Validation:** Safely checks video container headers using OpenCV `cv2.VideoCapture`.
2. **Frame Extraction:** Sequentially extracts video frames up to `max_frames` ($5$ to $50$ frames, default $20$ frames) and calculates physical timestamps.
3. **Frame Preprocessing:** BGR to RGB color management with immediate heap release.
4. **YOLO Object Detection:** Runs CPU inference using `models/yolo/yolo11n.pt` (confidence threshold $0.25$, NMS IoU $0.45$).
5. **ByteTrack Multi-Object Tracking:** Sequentially assigns persistent track IDs across frames using 2D Kalman state estimation.
6. **Multi-Modal Feature Extraction:** Extracts canonical 27-dimensional feature vectors covering detection counts, mean confidence, variance, blur (Laplacian variance), brightness, contrast, and motion displacement.
7. **Temporal Sequence Construction:** Formulates historical sequence tensors ($1 \times 10 \times 27$).
8. **Baseline Forecasting:** Generates persistence and rolling mean benchmark predictions.
9. **Multi-Horizon LSTM Forecasting:** Evaluates trained `LSTMForecaster` (loaded from `models/forecasting/lstm/model.pt`), predicting continuous future reliability for $H1, H3, H5 \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
10. **Reliability Risk Scoring:** Maps reliability into risk: $S_{t+h} = 1.0 - \hat{R}_{t+h}$.
11. **Risk Classification & Temporal Monitoring:** Ingests into `TemporalRiskMonitor`, computes EMA, trend velocity, and classifies into `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`.
12. **Early-Warning Alerts & Visualization:** `EarlyWarningEngine` triggers P1/P2/P3 alerts. Overlays bounding boxes, track IDs, confidences, and HUD risk badges onto frames, saving them to `outputs/jobs/{job_id}/annotated/`.
